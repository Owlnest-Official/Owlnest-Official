What is Lume?

Lume is OWLNEST’s first sleep-spectrum lamp, with light specially tuned for nighttime.

A soft silicone body rests on a wooden base. Turn it on with the button underneath.

See Lume
Specially tuned sleep spectrum

Designed around
the light’s composition

Two lamps can look equally warm and still have different spectra. Lume’s specially tuned sleep spectrum is designed with your natural melatonin rhythm in mind.

Using Lume

Light made for after dark

Keep Lume beside you for a few pages before bed, a late conversation, or a brief nighttime wake-up. Switch it off at bedtime unless you still need a little light.
