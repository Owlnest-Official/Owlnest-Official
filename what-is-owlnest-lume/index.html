Short Answer

What is Owlnest Lume?

Owlnest Lume is a sleep-supporting spectrum lamp for the hours before bed.

It is made for the time when you still need some light, but do not want the room to feel like daytime.

Products Check Your Fit
Night Light

Is Owlnest Lume a night light?

Not exactly. Most night lights are made for basic visibility. Lume is made for the pre-sleep light environment.

Warm Lamp

Is it just a warm lamp?

No. Warm-looking light is not automatically sleep-supporting. Lume uses a deep amber, low-blue spectrum for the hours before bed.

Timing

When should you use it?

Use Lume during the 1-2 hours before bed, while reading, getting ready, moving around, or lowering the room's stimulation.

Fit

Who may Owlnest Lume fit?

  • People who still need light before bed
  • People who dislike harsh room lights at night
  • People who want sleep support without apps or screens
  • People whose room still feels a little too awake
