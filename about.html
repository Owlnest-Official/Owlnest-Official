About Owlnest

Designed in Taiwan.
Inspired by Phu Quoc's Sunset.

Owlnest builds nighttime products around one idea: better light can help the room, and the body, move toward sleep.

Product Origin

Early physical sketch, before the final form.

Why We Started

Built from a simple question.

What if the light before bed was designed less for visibility, and more for helping the room feel ready for rest?

Why We Made Lume

Designing the quiet parts.

The compact form, USB-C charging, bottom-button control, and no-screen design were chosen to keep nighttime simple.
