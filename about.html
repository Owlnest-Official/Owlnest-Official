Why We Made Lume

Designing the quiet parts.

The compact form, USB-C charging, bottom-button control, and no-screen design were chosen to keep nighttime simple.

We are building Owlnest Lume because ordinary bedroom lighting is usually designed for visibility first. We wanted a simpler pre-sleep light: no app, no screen, no extra task, just a deep amber, low-blue glow designed for the hours before bed.
