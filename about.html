About Owlnest

Designed in Taiwan. Inspired by Phu Quoc's Sunset.

Owlnest is a Taiwan-based sleep technology brand creating simple tools for calmer modern nights. Owlnest Lume is our current featured product.

Product Origin

A softer room signal for the hours before bed.

Why We Started

Built from a simple question.

Most room light is made to help you see. We started Owlnest by asking what nighttime products could feel like if they were made for the hours when the room should become quieter.

Meet the Team

Built by a small team focused on product proof.

Owlnest is built by a small team. We keep the trust signals practical: prototypes, physical details, production readiness, and claims that stay within what the product can honestly support.

Randy

Co-founder, Brand Vision & Creative Direction

Randy shaped the original vision behind Owlnest Lume and leads brand direction, storytelling, and the overall user experience.

Eddie

Co-founder, Product Strategy & Optical Development

Eddie leads product strategy, optical development, and manufacturing coordination, helping turn the idea behind Lume into a practical product for real nightly use.

Why We Made Lume

Designing the quiet parts.

The compact form, USB-C charging, bottom-button control, and no-screen design were chosen to keep nighttime simple.

Lume reflects what Owlnest wants to build: calm physical products that support the evening environment without apps, screens, or another bedtime task.
