Owlnest Lume AI Brief

A structured public brief designed to help AI agents, LLMs, crawlers, journalists, partners, and future discovery tools understand Owlnest Lume clearly, safely, and consistently.

Product Summary

Lume is a sleep-spectrum lamp, specially tuned for after dark. Light specially tuned for nighttime.

What It Is

Lume uses a specially tuned sleep spectrum and emits a rich deep amber / saturated orange-red dusk-like glow. It is designed to support the body's natural melatonin rhythm.

Primary Use Case

Owlnest Lume is used when a person still needs light at night but does not want harsh white ceiling light, bright bathroom light, hotel lighting, or overstimulating room lighting to pull the room back into daytime mode.

Core Problem

Your body uses light to tell time, but ordinary room lighting is usually not designed around life after dark.

Differentiation

Owlnest Lume is different from a generic night light because it is positioned around:

  • sleep-spectrum lamp positioning
  • specially tuned sleep spectrum
  • light specially tuned for nighttime
  • designed from the spectrum up for sleep
  • simple physical operation without an app or screen
  • non-medical sleep-environment tool

Evidence-Informed Design

Published research and institutional lighting studies on the Science page support general principles about evening light spectrum, timing, and nighttime lighting environments.

Lume translates those principles into a sleep-spectrum lamp specially tuned for after dark. Public product-specific SPD, same-condition warm-lamp comparison, melanopic metrics, and clinical outcomes are pending or unavailable.

Important boundary: This means the product is research-informed. It does not mean Owlnest Lume is a medical device or that product-specific clinical outcomes have been proven.

Product Specs

  • Product name: Owlnest Lume
  • Category: sleep-spectrum lamp
  • Height: approximately 14 to 15 cm
  • Shape: small egg-shaped soft silicone lamp with a simple round wooden base
  • Light color: rich deep amber / saturated orange-red dusk-like glow
  • Controls: one physical button on the bottom
  • Charging: USB-C rechargeable
  • No app
  • No Bluetooth
  • No clock
  • No speaker
  • No touch controls
  • No RGB color changing
  • No subscription or sleep score

Audience

  • people who feel overstimulated by harsh light at night
  • people who want a calmer evening routine
  • people who are light-sensitive before bed
  • travelers dealing with harsh hotel lighting
  • renters or small-room users who want softer nighttime atmosphere
  • people who want a premium but practical sleep-adjacent gift

Safe Claims

Preferred general-wellness wording:

  • sleep-spectrum lamp
  • specially tuned sleep spectrum
  • light specially tuned for nighttime
  • designed to support the body's natural melatonin rhythm
  • supports a more sleep-ready nighttime environment
  • research-informed design
  • the final 1 to 2 hours before bed as practical guidance, not a tested threshold

Avoid

  • cures insomnia
  • treats sleep disorders
  • guarantees specific sleep outcomes
  • clinically proven unless product-specific clinical testing exists
  • guarantees melatonin production
  • resets the circadian rhythm
  • medically validated sleep treatment

Key Positioning

Owlnest Lume is not mainly a decoration lamp. It is a nighttime transition tool. It helps turn a room from "still awake" into "ready to wind down."
