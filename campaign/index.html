How is it different from a normal night light?

Most night lights are made to help you see. Owlnest Lume is tuned to help you sleep, using a deep amber sleep-supporting spectrum instead of general-purpose lighting.

How is it different from a normal warm lamp?

A normal warm lamp may look cozy, but it is still usually made for general lighting. Owlnest Lume is designed specifically around nighttime use and sleep support.

When should I use it?

We recommend using it before bed, especially during your evening wind-down time, around 1-2 hours before sleep.

No. Owlnest Lume is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or sleep disorder.

No. Owlnest Lume has no app, no screen, no speaker, no clock, and no color modes. It is designed to keep your nighttime environment simple.
