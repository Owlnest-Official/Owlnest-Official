The Problem

Most Lights Were Made for Seeing. Not Sleeping.

Your body reads light as a signal. Bright, blue-rich light is useful during the day, but at night it can keep your body more alert when it should be winding down.

Owlnest Lume was designed for the 1-2 hours before bed, when your room should stop acting like daytime. Its deep amber, low-blue spectrum helps reduce nighttime light disruption and supports your body’s natural sleep rhythm.
