Science / Beyond appearance

Color is only
part of the story.

What we see

Warmth.

Color describes how light looks.

What light contains

Spectrum.

Two warm-looking lights can have different spectral compositions. Appearance alone does not tell the whole story.

Light is also a signal.

Timing, intensity, duration and spectrum all matter to the body’s response to light. Lume starts with these research-informed design principles—not color alone.

General light research informs the design. Lume-specific biological or sleep outcomes require product-specific measurements and studies.
