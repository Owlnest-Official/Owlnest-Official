Folio 06 — The RoutineHow to use Owlnest Lume

06

How to use Owlnest Lume

Three simple steps.

  1. Place it near your bed

    On your nightstand, dresser, or hotel room table.

  2. Turn it on before sleep

    Let the room shift away from harsh daytime light.

  3. Turn it off at bedtime

    If you still need a little light during a brief night wake-up, turn it on again.
