Product Intro

A sleep-supporting spectrum lamp for the 1-2 hours before bed.

Owlnest Lume is not designed to make your room brighter. It is designed around the science of evening light: spectrum, timing, brightness, blue-rich wavelengths, and the body's natural nighttime rhythm. Its deep amber, low-blue spectrum helps reduce pre-sleep light disruption while your room shifts toward night.

Science Support

The spectrum matters.

Research shows that evening light spectrum, timing, and brightness can affect melatonin-related responses and circadian signaling. Owlnest Lume was designed around these principles: a deep amber, low-blue sleep-supporting spectrum for the hours before bed.

The research supports the broader lighting principles behind Lume. It is not product-specific clinical validation.
