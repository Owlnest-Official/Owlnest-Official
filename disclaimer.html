Support, safety, and policy terms

This policy reflects the current Owlnest site and direct-purchase flow. Shopify handles checkout, with available payment methods shown securely at checkout. Owlnest does not store payment information.

Key Points

  • Website content provides general information.
  • Orders begin on the Products page and continue through Shopify checkout.
  • Shipping charges and available delivery details are shown at Shopify checkout.
  • Owlnest does not provide medical advice or claim that Lume diagnoses or treats disease.
  • Owlnest products are not medical devices.
  • Research references inform design; they are not clinical validation of Lume.

1. Scope and Official Support

This Policy applies to Owlnest’s official website, official support channels, and products where Owlnest is the named brand owner or support contact.

Orders are processed through secure Shopify checkout. PayPal may be selected there when available. Owlnest does not store payment information.

For official support, warranty, returns, refunds, and product-use questions, contact team@owlnestofficial.com.

2. Product Use and Safety

Owlnest Lume is a sleep-spectrum lamp, specially tuned for after dark. It is not a bright task lamp or general-purpose room light.

Use only compatible charging accessories and power sources. Do not misuse the product, expose charging components to moisture, or attempt unauthorized modification, disassembly, or repair.

3. Non-Medical Statement

Owlnest does not provide medical advice or claim that Lume can diagnose, treat, or cure disease.

Owlnest products are not medical devices.

For medical concerns, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

4. Orders, Fulfillment, and Delivery

Owlnest Lume is available to order now. Choose Single or Duo on the Products page before secure Shopify checkout.

Delivery timing may depend on production, logistics, customs, and carriers. Delivery is generally confirmed by carrier records.

Returns, changes, and refunds follow the published return policy and payment-platform rules.
