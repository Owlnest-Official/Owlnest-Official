Before you buy

Is it bright enough for reading?

Lume is for winding down and moments that need a little light. It may suit brief reading, but is not a bright task lamp or the only safety light for stairs and obstacles.

How do I control and charge it?

Use the single physical button on the bottom and USB-C charging. No app, screen or touch control.

Can I leave it on all night?

At bedtime, turn Lume off unless a little light is still needed. We do not claim that leaving it on improves sleep or cannot affect melatonin.
