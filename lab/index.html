Owlnest Lab · Nighttime living research

Learning how life actually happens after dark.

Lab is Owlnest’s research ecosystem for understanding nighttime living—grounded in science, shaped by real homes, and open about what we still do not know.

Curiosity before certainty.
Why Lab exists

Theory explains. Real life reveals.

Owlnest designs products for nighttime living. But design alone is not enough. Science helps explain how light may matter after dark; everyday life shows how differently people actually use it.

Lab connects those two views. We study the foundations, observe real routines, and keep room for variation, uncertainty, and outcomes we did not expect.

The Lab ecosystem

Five parts. One learning system.

From established research to lived experience, each part has a different job—and none is permission to overstate what we know.

  1. 01

    Science

    The foundational layer: light spectrum, circadian timing, nighttime exposure, and how the body responds to light. It draws from general research, not product-specific proof.

    Foundational understanding
    Explore Science ↗
  2. 02

    Sleep Lab

    Real-world nighttime behavior: screen use, lighting environments, bedtime routines, irregular schedules, and the situations in which Lume naturally fits.

    Everyday observation, not clinical research
  3. 03

    21-Day Sleep Program

    A structured participant program for up to approximately 50 selected people in the United States. Participants use Lume in everyday life over 21 days and share feedback.

    Participant program · United States
    View the program ↗
  4. 04

    Participant Research

    How Owlnest learns from real people without turning their experiences into promises. Different habits, homes, and responses are part of the question.

    Honest feedback over manufactured proof
  5. 05

    Results

    A future record of what we actually observe, including limitations and variation. Research is ongoing, so there are no conclusions to publish yet.

    No data published yet
