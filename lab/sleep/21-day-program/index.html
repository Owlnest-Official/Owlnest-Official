Sleep Lab / 21-Day Sleep Program

21-Day
Sleep Program

7 prior nights of sleep records + 21 nights using Lume

Start the short application · about 1 minute

Applications are reviewed; selection is not guaranteed.

A home observation program for US adults with a sleep-tracking wearable. We want to understand how Lume fits ordinary life—including when it makes no noticeable difference.

Who can take part

Lume is free for selected participants

We cover the lamp and shipping. You may keep Lume after completing the program—or if you choose to withdraw. There is no return requirement, fee or penalty. Applying does not guarantee selection or a free product.

A sleep-tracking wearable is required

Applicants must be 18 or older and live in one of the 50 US states, including Alaska and Hawaii; shipping feasibility is checked before dispatch. Device eligibility depends on the sleep records it can provide. Recruitment is rolling, with a waitlist when places are full.

From application to participation

  1. Short application · about one minute

    Answer a short eligibility and sleep-background questionnaire.

  2. Initial review by our team

    Only invited applicants receive the separate full screening questionnaire.

  3. Full screening and final review

    Completing screening does not automatically mean acceptance.

  4. Confirm before dispatch

    Selected applicants reconfirm willingness, terms and a US delivery address.

  5. Begin on your first night

    Delivery is not Day 1. Your first night using Lume starts your own timeline.

  6. Share once a week

    Day 7, 14 and 21: a short check-in plus your wearable records.

7 baseline nights
+ 21 nights with Lume

Your baseline is the most recent 7 nights of existing wearable sleep records before your first use of Lume. You then record 21 nights using Lume: 28 nights of records in total, including the baseline.

Use Lume for about 60 minutes or more before bed, as the main ambient light where practical. Avoid concurrent bright cool-white lighting and keep your usual routine. No additional breathing exercises or training are required. Turn it off at bedtime unless light is needed.

This duration is the observation protocol, not a proven dose or promise of better sleep. Lume is not a medical device or replacement for medication. Do not change prescribed treatment for the program.

Your sleep records and consent

The required nightly fields are sleep date, sleep onset, wake time and total sleep time.

Optional measures depend on your device: sleep stages, awake time, heart rate, HRV or a sleep score. Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP and Samsung are examples; actual eligibility depends on usable records.

Send weekly screenshots or exports to the dedicated research mailbox provided after enrollment, and complete short Google Forms check-ins. Your study ID identifies the records; name and email are managed separately.

You have up to three days after each due date to submit. Missing required records exclude the participant from formal quantitative analysis, with the reason recorded—not from keeping Lume.

Withdrawal, retention and deletion

Withdrawal ends new data collection. Previously submitted information may be retained only within your consent and applicable law.

Identifiable raw study records are retained for at most two years from your study end, then deleted or irreversibly deidentified; truly anonymous aggregates may remain longer. A deletion request can be made through the study contact supplied at enrollment.

Research consent is not marketing permission

Stories, images and data need your review before publication. Social or paid advertising requires separate permission. You can decline marketing use and still keep Lume. Positive, unchanged and worse experiences must all be reported honestly.
