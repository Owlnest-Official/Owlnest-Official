About OWLNEST

Room for a change of pace

Life changes pace quickly. The body does not always follow on cue. OWLNEST exists to give those everyday transitions more thought—not another demand to keep up.

A question close to home

Why we started with the bedroom

Our founders noticed older people around them relying on sleep medication. It prompted a different question: what non-invasive approaches might help make room for natural sleep? Reading research on light and the body clock led to the first product, Lume. This was not a search for a replacement for prescribed care.

When the day moves faster than you do

You leave work, but work has not quite left you. You make time to focus, yet the morning still feels scattered. We want everyday objects to make room for the way people actually feel in those moments.

Working with the body’s own mechanisms

We start with how the body works, exploring light, sound, temperature and touch through products that ask little of the person using them. Research guides the design; product testing is needed to understand what it can actually do.

The first sketches

How Lume took shape

Lume made the first question tangible. The changing light of Phu Quoc remains part of its design inspiration. From nighttime light, OWLNEST’s mission grew toward natural transitions throughout the day—not a claim that one lamp can serve every state.
