Owlnest Lume Manual

Current product guide for Owlnest Lume.

This manual reflects the current product and campaign state of Owlnest Lume.
It is a sleep-supporting spectrum lamp designed for the 1 to 2 hours before bed, not a medical device and not a general-purpose bright room lamp.

Product Type

Sleep-supporting spectrum lamp

Recommended Use

Final 1 to 2 hours before bed

Light Range

1500K-1800K amber light

Purchase Path

Crowdfunding launch

Quick Specs

  • Size: 11 cm x 11 cm x 12 cm
  • Shape: egg-shaped diffuser with a simple round wooden base
  • Control: bottom power button
  • Charging: USB-C rechargeable
  • No app, Bluetooth, speaker, clock, projector, alarm, or color-changing modes

How to use Owlnest Lume

1. Place Owlnest Lume near your bedside area or another part of the room where it can support your evening environment without acting as bright task lighting.

2. Turn it on during the last 1 to 2 hours before bed, especially when you want the room to feel calmer and less like daytime.

3. Use it to replace brighter overhead light, harsh bedside lamps, or hotel lighting that keeps the room feeling active late at night.

4. Treat it as a repeatable environmental cue for nighttime wind-down, not as a promise of instant sleep results.

What Owlnest Lume is and is not

What it is
  • A sleep-supporting spectrum lamp.
  • A nighttime wind-down tool for the bedroom or evening routine.
  • A product designed around 1500K-1800K amber light and lower-stimulation nighttime use.
What it is not
  • Not a generic night light for simple visibility or navigation.
  • Not a medical device and not a treatment for insomnia or any sleep disorder.
  • Not a sunrise alarm, white noise machine, or app-based sleep gadget.

Safety and care

Use only compatible charging accessories and follow normal battery-safety precautions.

Do not disassemble the lamp or expose charging components to moisture.

Clean with a soft dry cloth.
Avoid harsh solvents or abrasive cleaning materials.

Keep the product away from extreme heat, prolonged direct water exposure, and situations where it may be crushed or sharply bent.

Warranty, returns, and campaign support

Campaign-backed units are expected to include a standard 1-year limited warranty for manufacturing defects after delivery.

Because the product is preparing for crowdfunding, refund timing, pledge adjustments, and fulfillment terms will follow the campaign and platform stage rather than normal instant-retail checkout rules.

For support, warranty questions, or product guidance, contact owlnestpq2025@gmail.com.

Important clarification

Owlnest Lume is designed around nighttime-friendly lighting principles and a calmer evening environment.
It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat sleep disorders, and should be understood as an environmental support product for the hours before bed.
