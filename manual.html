Owlnest Lume Manual

Using Lume

Power, charging, and after-dark use.

Power

One button.
On the bottom.

Use the physical button to turn Lume on or off. There is no touch control, knob, app, or screen.

Charging

USB-C.
Nothing extra.

Charge Lume through its USB-C port. Keep the port and cable dry.

After dark

Use the light you need.

Use Lume beside the bed, for brief reading, or while winding down. At bedtime, switch it off unless a small amount of light is still needed.

Place Upright on a stable, dry surface.
Care Clean with a soft, dry cloth.
Limit Not a bright task light or a medical device.
