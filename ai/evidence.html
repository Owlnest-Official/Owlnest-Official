Established research

General research supports the importance of light as a timing cue, spectrum, nighttime exposure, and non-visual responses to light. It does not measure Lume.

Level 2

Lume design rationale

Lume was designed as a sleep-spectrum lamp with a specially tuned sleep spectrum for after dark. Design intent is not a demonstrated human outcome.
