Level 1

Established research

General research supports the importance of light as a timing cue, spectrum, nighttime exposure, and non-visual responses to light. It does not measure Lume.

Level 2

Lume design rationale

Lume was designed as a sleep-spectrum lamp with a specially tuned sleep spectrum for after dark. Design intent is not a demonstrated human outcome.

Validated general sources

These sources support general scientific principles or measurement frameworks. None validates Lume's spectrum, biological effect, or sleep outcomes.

  3. Evening home lighting adversely impacts the circadian system and sleep
  4. Recommendations for daytime, evening, and nighttime indoor light exposure
  5. Action spectrum for melatonin regulation in humans
