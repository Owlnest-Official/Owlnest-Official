The Science

Why Spectrum Matters at Night

Light affects more than visibility. The spectrum you use before bed can influence how alert or sleep-ready your body feels.

The Blue-Rich Nighttime Problem

Why blue-rich light matters when the room should be slowing down.

Blue-rich light is especially relevant at night because it overlaps with the body’s light-sensitive pathways involved in alertness and circadian signaling.

Many ordinary white or warm-looking lamps are still designed mainly for visibility, and may contain blue-rich output that is less suitable for the hours before sleep.

Owlnest Lume is designed differently: a deeper amber, low-blue light direction for the hours before sleep.

General science background

The blue-cyan range around 480-495nm is commonly discussed as a nighttime alerting band relevant to circadian/light sensitivity. This is background context, not a disclosure of Owlnest Lume’s proprietary spectrum.
