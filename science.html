The Science

Why Spectrum
Matters at Night

Light affects more than visibility. The spectrum you use before bed can influence how alert or sleep-ready your body feels.

Nighttime Light Is Not Neutral

Not all warm light is sleep-supporting light.

Warm-looking light is not automatically sleep-supporting. The question is not only whether the light looks cozy, but how much blue-rich stimulation it brings into the hours before bed.

Regular warm light

Visibility first.

Warm-looking, but still designed mainly to help you see the room, often with more broad-spectrum output.

Owlnest Lume

Sleep spectrum first.

Deep amber, low-blue spectrum designed for the hours before sleep.
