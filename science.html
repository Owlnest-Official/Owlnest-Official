Science

Your body uses light to tell time.

Light helps the circadian system read day from night. After dark, timing, intensity, duration, and spectrum all matter.

Light Is More Than Color

Two warm-looking lights can have different spectra.

Appearance is how light looks. Spectrum is what the light contains.

Spectrum After Dark

Nighttime light is an environment, not just a color.

Different wavelengths and exposure conditions can send different timing cues after dark.
