---
name: Brand architecture
description: Owlnest’s approved relationship to Lume and the homepage content hierarchy.
---

Owlnest is the parent home-lifestyle brand for intentional nighttime living. Lume is its first product expression—a sleep-spectrum lamp—and may appear prominently, but must not become the entire brand story or homepage subject.

**Why:** Product-led messaging obscured what Owlnest is and left no natural place for future products. The user chose a nighttime ritual direction that first establishes the brand world, then introduces Lume as one way into it.

**How to apply:** On brand entry points, lead with Owlnest’s value to the evening, keep a single clear primary action, use concise copy, and progressively disclose product science and specifications. Leave visible conceptual space for future Owlnest objects; give Lume its detailed information on a focused product page.
