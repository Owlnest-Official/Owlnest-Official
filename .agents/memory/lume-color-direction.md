---
name: Lume color direction
description: Product-aligned color principles for Owlnest and Lume.
---

Owlnest should use an ink-black or blue-charcoal night environment with warm ivory for reading surfaces. Lume’s amber-orange to softly red light is the distinctive accent and should carry visual focus in product, CTA, and glow moments.

**Why:** A moss-green, parchment-led palette read as generic natural wellness or botanical living rather than the precise, tactile, modern nighttime product the user recognizes.

**How to apply:** Avoid using green as a dominant brand field. Let dark neutrals establish the brand space, use warm ivory for clarity, and keep the saturated warm light scarce enough that Lume remains the object that visibly changes the room.
