What is Owlnest Lume?
Why It Is Different

Made for sleep support, not just room visibility.

Regular warm lamps can look cozy while still being designed mainly for visibility. Lume is built around nighttime use and a simple physical experience.

Tuned light character

A deep amber, low-blue glow designed for nighttime use.

Natural night rhythm

Designed around the body’s response to evening light and darkness.

Simple by design

No app. No screen. No Bluetooth. A bottom-button control keeps the experience quiet.

Physical material proof

Soft silicone body, wooden base, USB-C charging, and compact bedside scale.

How to Use It

Use Lume as your main evening light before bed.

Turn down harsh ceiling lights earlier. Use Lume while reading, stretching, packing for tomorrow, or settling into your night.

02Use Lume as your evening light.
03Keep screens and bright light lower.
