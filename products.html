How to Use It

Use it when the room still needs a little light.

Use Lume after dark during wind-down before bed, or during brief nighttime wake-ups when a small amount of light is needed. At bedtime, switch it off unless a little light is still needed.

01Lower brighter, daytime-like room lighting.
02Turn on Lume during wind-down or a brief nighttime wake-up.
03At bedtime, switch it off unless a little light is still needed.
