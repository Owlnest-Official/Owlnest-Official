Sheet 06 — How To Use ItAfter dark

Plate IVReal night effect, photographed glow.

06

How to Use It

Use it when the room
still needs a little light.

Use Lume after dark during wind-down before bed, or during brief nighttime wake-ups when a small amount of light is needed. At bedtime, switch it off unless a little light is still needed.

  1. Lower brighter, daytime-like room lighting.

  2. Turn on Lume during wind-down or a brief nighttime wake-up.

  3. At bedtime, switch it off unless a little light is still needed.
