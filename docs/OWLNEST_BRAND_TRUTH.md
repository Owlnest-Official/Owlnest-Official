## 4. Research and evidence

Owlnest Sleep Lab is the only public Lab: a long-term real-life research system. The 21-Day Sleep Program is one method, not a separate Lab or clinical trial.

The purpose is product–user fit: who finds value, in which nighttime situations, where value is limited, and who may not fit. Retain positive, neutral, no meaningful change, and negative feedback.

Program timeline: Day 0 baseline; Day 7 and Day 14 check-ins; Day 21 review; Day 30 follow-up.

Internal rules:

- Do not present conceptual spectrum visuals as measured product data.
- General research informs design; it does not prove Lume-specific physiological or clinical outcomes.
- Publish measurements or participant findings only from real, verifiable records. Never fabricate participants, personas, testimonials, success rates, or comparison data.
- Product photographs demonstrate appearance and hardware, not spectrum or clinical efficacy.
- Keep application configuration intact. Public Program CTAs are informational; no recruitment numbers, application-status UI, or Results placeholders.

## 5. Claims and use

Use category, design, and environment language. Approved design intent: “Designed to support the body's natural melatonin rhythm.” / “設計用來支持身體自然的褪黑激素節律。”

Do not turn design intent into a measured effect. No diagnosis, treatment, insomnia cure, medical efficacy, clinical proof, guaranteed melatonin response, or guaranteed faster/deeper/longer sleep. No unsupported weight, metabolism, recovery, skin, anxiety, memory, baby-sleep, or fall-prevention outcomes.

Low-blue, no-blue, zero-blue, blue-light-free, blue-light-minimized, and night-tuned spectrum are retired as product positioning. Accurate attributed research may discuss wavelengths without defining Lume by them.

Use Lume after dark, while winding down, or for brief nighttime wake-ups. At bedtime, switch it off unless a small amount of light is needed. Do not promote all-night use as a sleep benefit. Keep essential safety guidance; do not invent numeric exposure rules.

Detailed hardware and safety boundaries: `docs/LUME_PRODUCT_TRUTH.md`.
