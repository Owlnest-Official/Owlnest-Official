## 1. Owlnest Brand Architecture

### Brand category

- English: **Nighttime living**
- Traditional Chinese: **夜晚生活**

Owlnest is a home-lifestyle brand made for life after dark.

Traditional Chinese:

Owlnest 是一個為夜晚生活而生的居家生活品牌。

### Brand purpose

Owlnest creates objects, spaces, and rituals for the part of home life that begins after dark.

The brand is not defined by one technology or one product. It is defined by a time of day and the way people want home to feel and function during that time.

### Official brand tagline

English:

> Made for life after dark.

Traditional Chinese:

> 為天黑後的生活而生。

This is the official Owlnest brand tagline. It defines the whole brand world, not only Lume.

Use it for:

- brand-level introductions
- the About page
- brand metadata when a brand tagline is needed
- brand films and manifestos
- future product-family contexts

Do not replace it with a Lume product line.

### Emotional brand line

English:

> Home begins after dark.

Traditional Chinese:

> 天黑之後，家才真正開始。

Use this selectively in:

- About and brand-story sections
- emotional editorial passages
- brand films
- brand manifestos

It is not the fixed logo tagline and must not be repeated on every page.

### Brand expansion territory

Owlnest may extend into:

- nighttime lighting
- scent
- bedding
- bedtime rituals
- home recovery spaces
- hotel and wellness experiences
- other objects designed for life after dark

### What Owlnest is not

Do not limit Owlnest to:

- a lamp brand
- a night-light brand
- a single sleep-technology brand
- a company name that exists only for Lume

Lume is the first hero product. It is not the full definition of Owlnest.

## 2. Message Ownership and Hierarchy

Each official line has one job. Do not interchange them.

| Layer | Official English | Official Traditional Chinese | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owlnest brand tagline | Made for life after dark. | 為天黑後的生活而生。 | Defines the Owlnest brand world |
| Owlnest emotional line | Home begins after dark. | 天黑之後，家才真正開始。 | Creates brand emotion and a picture of home after dark |
| Lume product tagline | Night deserves its own light. | 夜晚，值得有自己的光。 | Expresses Lume's product point of view |
| Lume Hero hook | Your room knows it’s night. Your light doesn’t. | 房間知道夜深了，燈卻還不知道。 | Grabs attention and names the nighttime-light mismatch |
| Lume product definition | Lume is a sleep-spectrum lamp, specially tuned for after dark. | Lume 是為天黑後特別調製的睡眠光譜燈。 | Explains what Lume is |
| Science bridge | Your body uses light to tell time. | 身體會從光，判斷現在是什麼時候。 | Connects the product problem to biology without teaching a full lesson |
| Advertising / comparison line | After dark, ordinary light gets it wrong. | 天黑之後，普通的燈就用錯了。 | Opens an ordinary-light comparison |

Before using any line, identify whether its job is brand, product point of view, attention, definition, science, comparison, evidence, or benefit.

Do not stack the brand tagline, product tagline, Hero hook, and product definition in one block. Give each section one job.

### Status of earlier lines

`Turn the day off, not the room.` may remain as secondary campaign language where it has a specific job, but it is not the official Owlnest brand tagline, the official Lume product tagline, or the primary Hero hook.

`Night deserves its own light.` belongs to Lume. Never use it as the Owlnest brand tagline.

`Home begins after dark.` is an emotional brand line. Never use it as a fixed logo tagline or repeat it mechanically.

## 3. Lume Product Architecture

### Role in the brand

Lume is Owlnest's first hero product.

### Formal category

- English: **sleep-spectrum lamp**
- Traditional Chinese: **睡眠光譜燈**

### Formal technical term

- English: **specially tuned sleep spectrum**
- Traditional Chinese: **特調睡眠光譜**

### Fixed plain-language explanation

- English: **Light specially tuned for nighttime.**
- Traditional Chinese: **為夜晚特別調製的光。**

Do not leave `sleep spectrum` or `睡眠光譜` unexplained at the first important consumer-facing use.

### Official product definition

English:

> Lume is a sleep-spectrum lamp, specially tuned for after dark.

Traditional Chinese:

> Lume 是為天黑後特別調製的睡眠光譜燈。

### Official product tagline

English:

> Night deserves its own light.

Traditional Chinese:

> 夜晚，值得有自己的光。

The line means:

- day and night should not automatically use the same light
- nighttime should not force a choice between harsh overhead light and total darkness
- Lume offers light designed specifically for after dark

### Primary Hero hook

English:

> Your room knows it’s night. Your light doesn’t.

Current Traditional Chinese spirit version:

> 房間知道夜深了，燈卻還不知道。

The Traditional Chinese may be refined later, but it must remain short, visual, contrasting, and distinctive. Do not turn it into a long explanatory sentence.

Do not flatten the Hero into generic phrases such as:

- Better light for better sleep
- A smarter way to relax
- Sleep better naturally
- The perfect bedside light

## 4. Lume Product Difference

Approved product education:

> Ordinary warm light only looks warm. Lume is designed from the spectrum up for sleep.

Traditional Chinese:

> 普通暖光只是看起來溫暖；Lume 從光譜開始為睡眠設計。

Approved spectrum explanation:

> Warm is how light looks. Spectrum is what the light contains.

Traditional Chinese:

> 暖，是光看起來的樣子；光譜，是光真正包含的內容。

These are product-education lines. They are not Owlnest or Lume taglines.

The intended distinction is not that Lume subtracts one component from an ordinary lamp. The intended distinction is that Lume creates light tuned specifically for after dark.

## 5. Science Compression System

Lume science must remain present without becoming a required course before purchase.

### Layer 1: Distinctive problem

> Your room knows it’s night. Your light doesn’t.

### Layer 2: Product answer

> Lume is a sleep-spectrum lamp, specially tuned for after dark.

### Layer 3: One-sentence science bridge

> Your body uses light to tell time.

Traditional Chinese:

> 身體會從光，判斷現在是什麼時候。

### Layer 4: Genuine scientific evidence

When available, evidence should include:

- real Lume spectral power distribution measurement
- a real ordinary warm-lamp comparison
- identical measurement conditions
- light distance
- actual illuminance or output condition
- light angle
- measurement method and equipment
- intended use context
- known limitations

The purpose of the visual is not to require consumers to interpret every value. It is to show at a glance that Lume differs in the composition of the light, not only its apparent color.

Never use:

- an AI-generated spectrum as evidence
- a concept illustration presented as measurement
- a polished curve without test conditions
- color temperature alone as proof of a sleep outcome
- general research presented as Lume-specific validation

## 6. Product Facts

### Fixed appearance

- small egg-shaped lamp body
- soft silicone material
- simple round wooden base
- approximately 14 to 15 cm tall
- deep amber, saturated orange-red, dusk-like glow
- nearby surfaces should visibly take on an orange-red cast

Do not depict Lume emitting:

- white light
- pale yellow light
- blue light
- purple light
- RGB color-changing light

### Fixed hardware

- USB-C charging
- one physical button on the bottom
- no App
- no screen
- no clock
- no speaker
- no Bluetooth
- no touch controls
- no projector
- no subscription
- no sleep score

Do not add or imply these features unless the repository, confirmed product specification, or user explicitly verifies them:

- adjustable brightness
- multiple output modes
- timer
- automatic dimming
- Sleep Mode
- Evening Mode

## 7. Approved Claims

Approved category, design, and general-wellness language:

- Sleep-spectrum lamp
- Specially tuned sleep spectrum
- Light specially tuned for nighttime
- Specially tuned for after dark
- Designed for nighttime
- Designed to support the body's natural melatonin rhythm
- Research-informed
- Supports a more sleep-ready nighttime environment
- Lume is a sleep-environment tool, not a medical device

Do not turn a design intention into a measured or guaranteed biological result.

## 8. Conditional Claims

The following require stronger product-specific evidence before prominent or affirmative use:

- Helps promote natural melatonin secretion
- Helps you fall asleep faster
- Helps you sleep longer
- Improves sleep quality
- Supports deeper sleep
- Supports stable sleep through the night

Until that evidence exists, use conservative design or environment language instead.

`low-blue`, `short-wavelength light`, and related terms may be discussed objectively in research context. They must not be restored as Lume's product name, category, headline, primary benefit, or main consumer positioning.

## 9. Prohibited Claims and Positioning

Do not say or imply that Lume:

- treats insomnia
- cures a sleep disorder
- guarantees better sleep
- guarantees faster sleep onset
- guarantees longer sleep
- guarantees an increase in melatonin
- is clinically proven to treat sleep problems
- is medical-grade sleep treatment
- is a medical device
- fixes or resets a circadian rhythm
- produces weight-loss, metabolism, appetite, muscle, skin, anxiety, memory, baby-sleep, or fall-prevention outcomes

Retired as primary Lume positioning:

- low-blue
- low-blue light
- low-blue sleep spectrum
- blue-light-minimized
- blue-light-free
- zero-blue
- no-blue
- no blue light
- 低藍光
- 無藍光
- 防藍光燈
- 藍光過濾燈

Even if future measurements show no meaningful blue output, do not make `no blue light` the main consumer positioning. It reduces Lume to one missing parameter rather than explaining the light that was created.

## 11. Overnight-Use Boundary

Do not combine these two propositions:

1. The product may physically remain on during sleep.
2. Keeping it on all night improves sleep or has no effect on melatonin.

An overnight scientific conclusion requires, at minimum:

- spectral power distribution
- melanopic EDI or equivalent metric
- actual illuminance
- use distance
- eye angle
- duration
- bedside position
- highest and lowest available output
- real room conditions

Current approved position:

- Use Lume before bed or during the night when a small amount of light is needed.
- Do not make all-night use a primary selling claim.
- Do not claim that leaving Lume on improves sleep, cannot affect sleep, or cannot affect melatonin.
- Preserve limitations until product-specific measurement conditions are available.

## 12. Brand Voice

Owlnest language must be:

- short
- clear
- observant
- contrasting
- visual
- slightly provocative
- scientific without sounding like a textbook
- premium without posturing
- intelligent without showing off
- purposeful, with one clear job per sentence

Avoid:

- generic healing language
- over-soft reassurance
- meditation-style filler
- solemn science lectures
- product-manager copy with no personality
- synthetic technology jargon
- large amounts of correct but forgettable copy
- language any night-light, diffuser, mattress, or meditation App could use unchanged

Before approving a line, ask:

1. Is this defining the brand, product, problem, difference, evidence, or benefit?
2. Is it repeating another line?
3. Would cutting it in half make it stronger?
4. Could a generic night-light, diffuser, mattress, or meditation App say the same thing?

If the answer to the fourth question is yes, the line is not specific enough to Owlnest.
