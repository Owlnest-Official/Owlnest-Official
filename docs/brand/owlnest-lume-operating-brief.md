# Owlnest Lume Operating Brief

Status: Short operating brief for future Codex runs, ChatGPT conversations, and AI tools.

Use this first for Owlnest Lume marketing, copy, ads, FAQ, creator materials, and Traditional Chinese work. This brief summarizes the rules; it does not replace the full source docs.

## 1. What Lume Is

English:

Owlnest Lume is a low-blue sleep-spectrum lamp designed to support natural melatonin and better sleep.

Traditional Chinese:

Owlnest Lume 是低藍光睡眠光譜燈，幫助促進自然褪黑激素分泌，讓身體進入更好睡的夜晚狀態。

## 2. What Lume Is Not

- not a normal night light
- not a decorative lamp
- not another sleep app
- not a medical device
- not an insomnia treatment
- not a weight loss lamp
- not a muscle recovery device
- not a skincare device
- not a productivity tool
- not a baby sleep product

## 3. Core Messaging

English:

- Turn the day off, not the room.
- Need light at night? Don’t bring daytime back.
- Not a normal night light. A sleep-spectrum lamp.
- Deep amber, low-blue light for better nights.
- Support melatonin. Keep the room usable. Sleep better.

Traditional Chinese:

- 關掉白天，不必關掉整個房間
- 晚上需要一點光，但不需要把白天帶回房間
- 不是普通小夜燈，是睡眠光譜燈
- 深琥珀、低藍光，為睡眠設計
- 幫助促進自然褪黑激素分泌，保留夜晚需要的光，睡得更好

## 4. Taiwan Traditional Chinese Tone Rules

- Write naturally for Taiwan readers.
- Avoid direct English translation.
- Avoid mainland-style wording.
- Avoid 「支持」 in consumer-facing Chinese copy.
- Use 「幫助促進 / 幫助 / 讓...更...」 where natural.
- Do not make the Chinese sound like medical supplement copy.
- Keep claims clear, sales-oriented, and consumer-friendly.

Preferred Traditional Chinese wording:

- 睡前光線
- 夜晚光線
- 夜晚柔和光線
- 幫助促進自然褪黑激素分泌
- 幫助身體進入夜晚模式
- 幫助睡得更好
- 讓房間保留剛剛好的光
- 不用全黑，也不會亮得像白天
- 晚上需要一點光，但不需要把白天帶回房間

## 5. Forbidden / Risky Claims

Avoid:

- cure / treatment / guarantee claims
- weight loss / fat burn / metabolism / appetite claims
- muscle growth / recovery outcome claims
- skin repair / anti-aging outcome claims
- anxiety / mental health treatment claims
- memory improvement claims
- baby sleep outcome claims
- fall prevention claims

Do not make Lume sound like a disease treatment, clinical device, or guaranteed biological result.
