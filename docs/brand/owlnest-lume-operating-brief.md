## 1. Owlnest Brand

Owlnest is a home-lifestyle brand for **Nighttime living** / **夜晚生活**.

Official Owlnest brand tagline:

> Made for life after dark.

> 為天黑後的生活而生。

Selective emotional line:

> Home begins after dark.

> 天黑之後，家才真正開始。

Owlnest is not limited to lamps, night lights, sleep technology, or Lume. Lume is the first hero product, not the entire brand.

## 2. Lume Product

Official product category:

- English: **sleep-spectrum lamp**
- Traditional Chinese: **睡眠光譜燈**

Official product definition:

> Lume is a sleep-spectrum lamp, specially tuned for after dark.

> Lume 是為天黑後特別調製的睡眠光譜燈。

Technical term and required plain-language explanation:

- **specially tuned sleep spectrum** / **特調睡眠光譜**
- **Light specially tuned for nighttime.** / **為夜晚特別調製的光。**

Do not leave `sleep spectrum` or `睡眠光譜` unexplained at its first important consumer-facing use.

## 4. Product Difference

Approved warm-light explanation:

> Ordinary warm light only looks warm. Lume is designed from the spectrum up for sleep.

> 普通暖光只是看起來溫暖；Lume 從光譜開始為睡眠設計。

Approved spectrum explanation:

> Warm is how light looks. Spectrum is what the light contains.

> 暖，是光看起來的樣子；光譜，是光真正包含的內容。

The product difference is the light designed for after dark, not a claim that Lume is simply an ordinary lamp with one component removed.

## 5. Science Compression

Use this sequence:

1. Name the nighttime-light mismatch.
2. Define Lume in one sentence.
3. Use one science bridge: `Your body uses light to tell time.`
4. Show real, conditioned product evidence when it exists.
5. Offer the Science page for deeper reading.

Do not make visitors complete a lesson on wavelength, ipRGCs, melanopic EDI, melatonin, and sleep before they understand the product.

Do not remove science. Major product pages still need a category anchor, a nighttime-tuning explanation, a conservative physiological design statement or research bridge, and a Science-page entry.

## 6. Claims

Approved conservative language:

- Sleep-spectrum lamp
- Specially tuned sleep spectrum
- Light specially tuned for nighttime
- Designed to support the body's natural melatonin rhythm
- Research-informed design
- Helps create a calmer nighttime environment
- Helps the body move into a more sleep-ready state
- Supports a more sleep-ready nighttime environment

Requires stronger product-specific evidence before affirmative use:

- helps promote natural melatonin secretion
- helps people fall asleep faster
- helps people sleep longer
- improves sleep quality
- supports deeper or more stable sleep
- claims that leaving Lume on all night improves sleep or cannot affect melatonin

Prohibited:

- insomnia treatment or cure
- guaranteed faster, longer, deeper, or better sleep
- guaranteed melatonin production
- clinically proven or medically validated without actual product testing
- medical-device or medical-grade treatment positioning
- weight, metabolism, appetite, muscle, skin, anxiety, memory, baby-sleep, or fall-prevention outcomes

Lume is a sleep-environment tool, not a medical device.

## 7. Retired Positioning

Do not use these as Lume's product name, category, headline, metadata identity, or first-sentence definition:

- low-blue / low blue / 低藍光
- no-blue / zero-blue / 無藍光
- blue-light-free / blue-light-minimized
- night-tuned spectrum

Accurately attributed research may still discuss blue-rich light, short-wavelength light, or a study's actual spectral condition. Do not turn that research language back into Lume's consumer identity.

## 8. Usage and Evidence

- Use Lume after dark, during the wind-down period before bed, or during brief nighttime wake-ups when some light is needed.
- `1 to 2 hours before bed` is practical guidance, not a tested threshold or universal requirement.
- At bedtime, switch Lume off unless a small amount of light is still needed.
- Do not promote continuous all-night use as a sleep benefit.
- Do not claim a fixed usage distance, angle, illuminance, CCT, melanopic value, or spectral result without real product records.

Current product-specific SPD, warm-lamp comparison, illuminance conditions, melanopic metrics, and clinical sleep outcomes are **pending**.

General studies support design principles only. Product photographs support appearance and hardware facts only. Conceptual diagrams must never be labeled as product measurements.

## 9. Verified Product Facts

- small egg-shaped soft-silicone lamp body
- simple round wooden base
- approximately 14 to 15 cm tall
- deep amber, saturated orange-red glow
- nearby surfaces take on an orange-red cast
- USB-C charging
- one physical button on the bottom
- no App, screen, clock, speaker, Bluetooth, touch control, RGB, projector, subscription, or sleep score

Do not add brightness adjustment, multiple modes, a timer, automatic dimming, or another unverified feature.

## 10. Traditional Chinese Voice

- Avoid generic wellness filler and mainland-style wording.
- Avoid generic consumer-facing uses of `支持`. The exact approved statement `設計用來支持身體自然的褪黑激素節律` is an intentional exception.
