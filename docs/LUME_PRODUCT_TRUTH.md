## 1. Product Definition

### Brand

Owlnest is a nighttime-living home-lifestyle brand.

- Brand category: **Nighttime living**
- Official brand tagline: **Made for life after dark.**

Traditional Chinese:

Owlnest 是一個為夜晚生活而生的居家生活品牌。

- 品牌領域：**夜晚生活**
- 正式品牌主句：**為天黑後的生活而生。**

### Formal category

- English: **sleep-spectrum lamp**
- Traditional Chinese: **睡眠光譜燈**

### Formal technical language

- English: **specially tuned sleep spectrum**
- Traditional Chinese: **特調睡眠光譜**

Always pair the technical term with a plain-language explanation near its first important use:

- English: **Light specially tuned for nighttime.**
- Traditional Chinese: **為夜晚特別調製的光。**

Do not leave `sleep spectrum` or `睡眠光譜` unexplained in a hero, product definition, comparison, or first-time consumer introduction.

### Approved one-sentence definition

English:

> Lume is a sleep-spectrum lamp, specially tuned for after dark.

Traditional Chinese:

> Lume 是為天黑後特別調製的睡眠光譜燈。

### Approved physiological design statement

English:

> Designed to support the body's natural melatonin rhythm.

Traditional Chinese, as explicitly approved in the 2026-07-17 handoff:

> 設計用來支持身體自然的褪黑激素節律。

This newer approved Traditional Chinese terminology supersedes the older files that prohibit `支持`. Do not replace it with a stronger biological-outcome claim.

### What Lume is not

Lume is not:

- a normal night light
- an ordinary warm lamp
- a mood lamp or decorative lamp
- a sunset lamp
- an RGB lamp
- a red-light therapy device
- a medical device
- a smart sleep alarm
- a sleep tracker
- a treatment for insomnia or any sleep disorder

## 3. Claim Ladder

### Level 1: Human problem and room feeling

Approved for prominent use:

- Your room knows it’s night. Your light doesn’t.
- Your ceiling light still thinks it's daytime.
- Your room isn't ugly. The light is wrong.
- Night still needs some light, but it does not need daytime brought back into the room.
- Helps create a calmer nighttime environment.
- Helps the body move into a more sleep-ready state.

`Turn the day off, not the room.` may remain as secondary campaign language, but it is not the official Owlnest brand tagline, Lume product tagline, or primary Hero hook.

### Level 2: Category and product distinction

Approved for prominent use:

- Night deserves its own light.
- Lume is a sleep-spectrum lamp, specially tuned for after dark.
- Not a night light. A sleep-spectrum lamp.
- Lume is light specially tuned for nighttime.
- Ordinary warm light only looks warm. Lume is designed from the spectrum up for sleep.
- Warm is how light looks. Spectrum is what the light contains.

### Level 3: Physiological design intent

Approved with `designed to`, `supports`, or an equally conservative qualifier:

- Designed to support the body's natural melatonin rhythm.
- Supports a more natural evening wind-down.
- Research-informed design.
- Evidence-informed circadian lighting.

Do not turn a design intention into a guaranteed biological result.

### Level 4: Product evidence

Approved only when supported by real product-specific records:

- measured Lume spectral power distribution
- measured ordinary warm-lamp comparison
- measurement conditions, distance, angle, output setting, equipment, and date
- product-specific illuminance or melanopic metrics

Current status: **pending**. No public product-specific spectral report is present in the repository.

### Level 5: Sleep outcome

Use only as conservative general-wellness language:

- designed for better nights
- helps create a more sleep-ready nighttime setting
- helps the room feel ready for sleep
- designed to help people wind down before sleep

Do not promise faster sleep onset, longer sleep, deeper sleep, stable sleep, or a fixed improvement for every person.

## 5. Conditional Claims

These claims require the stated context and must not be used as unsupported hero claims.

### Melatonin secretion

`Helps promote natural melatonin secretion` / `幫助促進自然褪黑激素分泌` may appear only in longer science or FAQ context that:

- explains that the statement is a design intention informed by general light-at-night research
- uses `designed to`, `helps`, or equivalent conservative wording
- does not claim that Lume has measured a hormone change in users
- does not promise a fixed result for every person

Preferred primary wording remains:

> Designed to support the body's natural melatonin rhythm.

### Low-blue terminology

`low-blue`, `low blue`, and `低藍光` are retired as formal product positioning and must not define Lume in a hero, product title, category name, metadata summary, or first-sentence product description.

They may appear only:

- as general background science about blue-rich versus less blue-rich nighttime light; or
- as a product-specific technical characteristic after a real spectral measurement validates the wording.

Until product-specific validation exists, do not use a quantitative blue-light reduction claim.

### Overnight or all-night light

All-night use is **not an approved primary selling claim**.

Approved conditional meaning:

- Use Lume before bed or during the night when a small amount of light is still needed.
- A person who does not want total darkness may leave a small, soft light in the room according to personal comfort.
- Individual rooms and light sensitivities vary.

Do not say that Lume is `made to stay on while you sleep`, that leaving it on improves sleep, or that it does not affect sleep.

### Better sleep

`Better sleep` may be used only as an intended general-wellness direction, paired with design or environment language. Do not present it as a measured product outcome.

## 6. Prohibited Claims

Do not use any claim that says or implies Lume:

- cures insomnia
- treats, cures, diagnoses, or prevents a sleep disorder
- is clinically proven without actual product-specific clinical testing
- guarantees faster sleep, deeper sleep, longer sleep, or better sleep
- guarantees melatonin production or raises melatonin in every person
- fixes or resets the circadian rhythm
- is medically validated or medical-grade treatment
- causes weight loss, burns fat, boosts metabolism, or controls appetite
- builds muscle or guarantees recovery
- repairs skin or provides anti-aging outcomes
- treats anxiety or another mental-health condition
- improves memory
- makes babies sleep better
- prevents falls

Prohibited technical positioning:

- no-blue / no blue light / 無藍光
- zero-blue / zero blue light
- blue-light-minimized as a formal product definition
- exact blue-light reduction percentages without real measurement
- an AI-generated or conceptual spectrum shown as measurement data
- a general research paper presented as product-specific proof

## 7. Usage Facts

### Recommended timing

Primary use:

- after dark when ordinary room lighting feels too daytime-like
- during the wind-down period before bed, including the final 1 to 2 hours as practical guidance
- during brief nighttime wake-ups when a small amount of light is needed

The `1 to 2 hours` window is usage guidance, not a tested threshold and not a promise that every person must use Lume for that exact duration.

At bedtime, switch Lume off unless a small amount of light is still needed. Do not promote continuous overnight use as a sleep benefit.

### Recommended distance

No verified numeric distance is established in the repository.

- Do not publish a fixed distance until product-specific illuminance and spectrum measurements define the test and use conditions.
- Do not invent bedside-distance numbers.

### Recommended placement

- Place Lume upright near the bed or another low-light nighttime area.
- Use a stable, dry, level surface.
- Keep it away from water, open flame, heaters, direct heat, and fabric that could cover the lamp.
- Position it so the room receives the needed soft light without treating it as bright overhead or task lighting.
- Use additional lighting where steps, obstacles, hazards, or detailed tasks require clear visibility.

No verified consumer rule for angle, eye level, or indirect placement is currently established.

### Reading and navigation

- Lume may provide enough soft light for simple cues, brief reading, or settling down.
- It is not a bright task lamp.
- It must not be described as the sole safety light for stairs, obstacles, or other hazards.
