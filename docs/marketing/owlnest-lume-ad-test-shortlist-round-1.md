Main positioning:

English:

Owlnest Lume is a low-blue sleep-spectrum lamp designed to support natural melatonin and better sleep.

Traditional Chinese:

Owlnest Lume 是低藍光睡眠光譜燈，幫助促進自然褪黑激素分泌，讓身體進入更好睡的夜晚狀態。

## 1. Round 1 Testing Goal

This shortlist is for early creative testing. The goal is not to prove a full campaign strategy yet. The goal is to identify which adjacent pain points earn attention while keeping Owlnest Lume's product meaning clear.

Round 1 should test whether people respond more strongly to:

* nighttime phone and room light tension
* fitness recovery routines
* weight management routines
* skincare and beauty sleep routines
* productivity and brain recovery routines

Traffic should go to the Products page first. Do not build dedicated landing pages yet.

Winning hooks can later become:

* campaign pages
* creator prompts
* UGC scripts
* retargeting angles
* email subject lines
* product education modules

## 2. Test Setup Recommendation

* Use Meta and TikTok first.
* Use simple static, text-on-video, or UGC-style creative.
* Keep the product explanation short.
* Show Lume as a deep amber, low-blue sleep-spectrum lamp.
* Send traffic to the Products page.
* Measure CTR, landing page view, preorder click, discount code usage, and creator link performance if applicable.
* Do not optimize only for likes or comments.
* Do not make the creative feel like a medical, weight loss, beauty, muscle growth, or productivity promise.

## 3. The 10 Hooks

### R1-01

Angle: Nighttime phone use / screen fatigue

English hook:

Your phone has night mode. Does your room?

Traditional Chinese hook:

手機開了夜間模式，但你的房間還沒

One-line body copy in English:

Lume brings deep amber, low-blue sleep-spectrum light into the room so bedtime does not still feel like daytime.

One-line body copy in Traditional Chinese:

Lume 把深琥珀、低藍光睡眠光譜帶進房間，讓睡前不再像白天還沒結束。

CTA in English:

See Owlnest Lume

CTA in Traditional Chinese:

了解 Owlnest Lume

Why this hook is worth testing:

This is the closest bridge to the product. People already understand phone night mode, blue light, and screen habits, so the room-level extension should be intuitive.

Claim risk level: Low

Notes for avoiding risky edits:

Do not claim Lume removes all blue light or protects eyesight. Keep the claim about room light, sleep-spectrum design, and nighttime blue-light exposure.

## 4. Top 3 Recommended Launch Order

### 1. R1-01: Your phone has night mode. Does your room?

Why:

This is the closest angle to the product. It connects directly to blue light, night mode, screen habits, and the idea that the room needs a better nighttime signal.

### 2. R1-04: You are not undertraining. You may be under-resting.

Why:

Fitness recovery has strong buying power and gives the ad a clear emotional tension. It should be tested carefully because the copy must avoid direct recovery or performance claims.

### 3. R1-08: Skincare does not end when the serum dries.

Why:

This angle can look premium in static, UGC, and creator content. It borrows attention from beauty routines while returning safely to sleep-ready room light.

Backup launch option:

R1-06 can replace R1-04 if the team wants to test stronger pain earlier, but it requires stricter review because weight management has higher claim risk.

## 5. Red Line Checklist

Before launch, confirm:

* Does the hook borrow attention without changing product positioning?
* Does the body copy return to sleep-spectrum light?
* Does the copy mention sleep, bedtime light, blue light, melatonin, night mode, or the room signal?
* Does the copy avoid direct outcome claims?
* Does it avoid medical, weight loss, beauty, muscle growth, productivity, and mental health claims?
* Does the Traditional Chinese avoid stiff translated wording and prohibited terms?
* Does the CTA send people to learn about Lume rather than promising a result?
* Would the ad still be accurate if the product is understood only as a low-blue sleep-spectrum lamp?
