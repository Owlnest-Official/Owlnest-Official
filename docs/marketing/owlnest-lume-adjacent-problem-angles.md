# Owlnest Lume Adjacent Problem Angles

Status: Internal marketing strategy reference
Use for: ad strategy, hook testing, creator briefs, email angles, blog planning, campaign planning
Do not treat this as public page copy by default. This is an internal strategy document.

## 1. Strategy Summary

Owlnest Lume should not only market to people who are already searching for sleep lamps.

The stronger opportunity is to borrow attention from adjacent problems people already care about and already spend money on: weight management, fitness recovery, skincare, productivity, stress recovery, studying, screen habits, parenting, health routines, and nighttime safety.

The common bridge is:

> You may be missing the night layer.

Many people already optimize the daytime part of a problem. They buy supplements, gym memberships, skincare, productivity tools, screen settings, routines, and apps. But the night layer is often unfinished: light, timing, bedroom signals, and the way the body transitions out of daytime mode.

Owlnest Lume should not claim to solve every life problem directly. Lume's role is narrower and stronger:

Owlnest Lume helps make the night layer more sleep-friendly through deep amber, low-blue sleep-spectrum light.

Main positioning:

English:

Owlnest Lume is a low-blue sleep-spectrum lamp designed to support natural melatonin and better sleep.

Traditional Chinese:

Owlnest Lume 是低藍光睡眠光譜燈，幫助促進自然褪黑激素分泌，讓身體進入更好睡的夜晚狀態。

## 2. Non-Negotiable Positioning Boundary

Lume is not:

* a weight loss lamp
* a muscle recovery device
* a skincare product
* a productivity tool
* a mental health treatment
* a baby sleep product
* a medical device

Lume is:

* a low-blue sleep-spectrum lamp
* a deep amber night light for better sleep
* a way to keep the room usable without bringing daytime back
* a better night signal for people who need light after dark

Do not stretch the product into a cure, treatment, performance enhancer, or guaranteed outcome. The adjacent angle earns attention; the product promise stays about sleep-spectrum light, melatonin-friendly nighttime signals, and better bedtime light.

## 4. Recommended Priority Order

1. Nighttime phone use / screen fatigue
2. Fitness recovery
3. Weight management
4. Skincare / beauty sleep
5. Productivity / brain recovery

Why this order:

* Nighttime phone use is closest to the product. People already understand night mode, blue light, screens, and bedtime habits. The bridge from phone to room is intuitive.
* Fitness recovery has strong sleep logic and strong buying power. It can be framed around recovery without claiming direct muscle outcomes.
* Weight management has the strongest pain and largest market, but also the highest claim risk. Use carefully and always route the claim back to sleep and nighttime routine.
* Skincare has strong visual and social content potential. The "beauty sleep" bridge is familiar, but the product must not become a skincare product.
* Productivity has brand quality and founder-story potential. It can make Owlnest feel smarter and more premium, as long as it avoids performance guarantees.

## 7. Suggested Testing Plan

Start small. Do not build full landing pages before an angle has evidence.

Phase 1: Hook testing

* Start with 3 ad angles: nighttime phone use, fitness recovery, and skincare / beauty sleep.
* Use Meta and TikTok hooks first.
* Send traffic to the Products page first.
* Keep the core product page positioning unchanged.
* Track CTR, product page engagement, preorder clicks, discount code usage, creator link performance, and checkout initiation.

Phase 2: Angle validation

* Compare hooks by click quality, not just CTR.
* Watch whether people use discount codes or creator links after clicking each angle.
* Review comments and DMs for language customers naturally repeat.
* Identify which angle creates desire without forcing unsafe claims.

Phase 3: Build only after traction

* Build one lightweight campaign page only after one angle shows clear traction.
* Keep checkout claims neutral and product-focused.
* Do not put weight management claims into checkout.
* Keep the homepage positioning stable until repeated data shows a broader repositioning is useful.
